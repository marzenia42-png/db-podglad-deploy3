An office that changes with the company

An office is bought for years. A company changes every quarter — new people join, teams merge, some work hybrid. Orgatec 2026 in Cologne says it plainly: change is the new reality. Here is how to buy furniture that can handle that change — without rebuilding and without replacing everything after three years.

DB Meble guide · ~5 min read

Orgatec 2026: change as the starting point

Orgatec 2026 takes place on 27–30 October in Cologne. For the first time in its history the fair has a central theme: "From rooms to relationships: designing spaces in an ever-changing world".

One of the two megatrends of this edition is "Transition". The organiser writes that "change is the new reality" and sees transition competence as a key feature of forward-thinking organisations. Workspaces should be designed with the flexibility that this change requires.

For a company buying furniture, this is a very practical question: can what goes into the office today be moved, extended or used in a different zone two years from now?

Three zones instead of one layout

Bejot, a Polish manufacturer we work with, will present a stand themed be:together in Cologne (hall 7.1, stand D020/E021). It describes the office as a system of relationships, divided into three zones:

  • be:balanced — focus and rest, few stimuli,
  • be:comfortable — confidential talks, meetings and teamwork with moderate stimuli,
  • be:connected — intensive collaboration and exchange, many stimuli.

The manufacturer stresses that no zone is more important than another. The takeaway for purchasing: instead of a single desk-next-to-desk layout, plan several types of places — and furniture that can move between them.

Furniture that can be moved

The simplest flexibility test: does rearranging a room require an installation crew?

  • Haali — lightweight conference chairs with seven base types, including swivel versions on castors for workshops. The collection also includes acoustic screens and a table, ordered as one set. More: Bejot Haali.
  • Multiwall — a mobile screen with a magnetic whiteboard surface on a metal frame, 1400 or 1710 mm wide and 1900 mm high. It separates a work zone in an open space and carries the team's notes between meetings. More: Bejot Multiwall.
  • Quadra — acoustic booths the manufacturer calls "a room within a room". The PB-R version has transport castors, so a phone booth can be moved when the office layout changes. More: Bejot Quadra.

Systems that grow with the team

The second test: will it be possible to buy more pieces of the same line in a few years?

  • Linje (design: Kasper Mose) — modular soft seating whose modules connect into lines that lead through the space. It was originally designed for education, where furniture must withstand intensive use and quickly change layout. Straight modules are 750, 1500 and 2250 mm wide. More: Bejot Linje.
  • Fora Office — a modular system for meeting and informal work zones: 15 elements in the family, tables included. Modules can be added. When buying more years later, it is worth checking fabric availability, as the colour may differ slightly between batches. More: Bejot Fora Office.
  • Saar — a modular steel shelving system for storage, dividing an open space, hanging clothes and seating. It can stand freely as a partition, and the HANG version is wall-mounted. The code describes the module grid — 3X5 means 3 columns and 5 rows. More: Bejot Saar.

All collections listed except Quadra carry a 5-year manufacturer's warranty on our product pages.

5 questions before buying furniture for an office that will change

  1. Can workstations be moved without an installation crew?
  2. Will you be able to buy the same collection in a few years?
  3. Is the system modular — can it be extended with more workstations?
  4. What is the manufacturer's warranty and what does it cover?
  5. Will the furniture work in a different office layout — open space, meeting room, quiet zone?

If the answer to three of the five questions is "I don't know", it is worth pausing before ordering.

FAQ

What does the "transition" trend at Orgatec 2026 mean? It is one of the two megatrends of the fair. The organiser treats change as a permanent state and the ability to move through it as a feature of forward-thinking organisations. For furniture it means flexibility: rearranging, extending, changing a zone's function.

Can shelving replace a partition wall? The Saar shelving has a steel structure and can stand freely as a partition in an open space. The HANG version is wall-mounted.

Can an acoustic booth be moved? In the Quadra family, the PB-R version has transport castors. The other versions stand on a static base.

What warranty do the Bejot products in this article have? Haali, Multiwall, Linje, Fora Office and Saar carry a 5-year manufacturer's warranty. Terms for Quadra booths are provided with the quote.
