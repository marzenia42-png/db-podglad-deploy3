How does CAFE VII HOCKER differ from CAFE VII UP&DOWN?+
CAFE VII HOCKER is a fixed-height stool on a skid base. CAFE VII UP&DOWN is a column stool with height adjustment (gas lift) on a chromed base — for counters of varying height.
What is the shell finish?+
Your choice of: a wood shell (beech plywood stained to colour), laminated wood in a colour from the laminate palette, or fully upholstered (A-PLUS option) in a fabric from the Nowy Styl price groups.
What colours is the frame available in?+
The Cafe VII Hocker frame is chromed or powder-coated: White Aluminium RAL 9006, Traffic White RAL 9016, Anthracite Grey RAL 7016, Vermilion Red RAL 030 50 40, Ochre Yellow RAL 1024, Cashmere NCS S 2005-Y60R, Violet Blue RAL 5000, Olive Green RAL 6003; standard Jet Black RAL 9005.
Do the bar stools match the Cafe VII chairs?+
Yes — it is the same Cafe VII line. Bar stools at the high counter and conference chairs at the table keep a consistent aesthetic in one space.
What is the lead time?+
The lead time depends on the configuration and quantity — we confirm the exact date on the day the enquiry or order is placed.
Where do the Cafe VII bar stools work well?+
In staff cafés, canteens and break-out areas at high counters, and at reception desks — anywhere people sit at a raised counter.
